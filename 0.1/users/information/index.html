    Your information¶

    Your information shows what the sign-in service of your organisation holds about you, and lets you correct what your organisation opens to you. Change history, beside the title, lists what changed and who changed it.

    What the page shows¶

    The page shows what your account holds in two parts.

    • Your account: your Username, when the account was created, Account created, and when it last changed, Last change. Nobody changes these here.
    • About you: one line per detail, such as Email address, Phone number, Full name, Given name, Family name, Date of birth, Language, Time zone or Postal address. An email address and a phone number say whether they were confirmed: verified or not verified. Nothing yet. means your account holds no detail about you.

    Correcting a detail¶

    Your organisation decides which details you may correct. They can include your names, Nickname, Preferred username, Date of birth, Gender, Language, Time zone, Profile page, Picture, Website and Phone number. Your username, your email address and your postal address are never changed here; ask your organisation. When you sign in through your organisation's own sign-in service, the details it fills cannot be changed here either: they follow what your organisation holds.

    A detail you may correct is a field. Type in it, and press Save, which appears beside the field while you point at it or type in it. Empty a field to remove it. Date of birth takes a full date; when your account holds only the year, the field takes text instead, written like 1815-12-10, or 1815. Profile page, Picture and Website take a full web address, starting with https:// or http://. Any other detail is one line of at most 255 characters, and a phone number of at most 64. A value the field does not accept is answered This value is not valid.

    When there is nothing you may correct, the page says This information cannot be changed here.

    What you choose under Language is the language of the pages of your account and of the messages sent to you. Time zone is the zone every date of your account is written in.

    Change history¶

    Change history lists, for each change: the Detail, what it said Before, what it says After, When it changed, and By: You, or An administrator of your organisation, who is never named. No change yet. means nothing changed. Changes are kept for a period your organisation sets.

    Confirming an email address or a phone number¶

    An email address or a phone number that is not verified receives no notification from the sign-in service. When your organisation can send you email or text messages, the page lists such addresses under Addresses to confirm, with Until an address is confirmed, it receives only its confirmation code. and Send a code beside each.

    1. Press Send a code. The page Confirm your email address, or Confirm your phone number, says A code was sent to the address, Enter it here or open the link that came with it.
    2. The message says Your confirmation code: followed by six digits and the moment it stops being valid. Type the code under Code and press Confirm. Or open the link in the message: its page says Confirm that this address is yours., and Confirm this address confirms it. The link needs you signed in on that browser.
    3. The address is then marked verified.

    A code sent by email is valid for 24 hours, a code sent by text message for 10 minutes. Five wrong codes end it, and Send another code replaces it with a new one. If you did not ask for a code, ignore the message.

    The number of codes sent is limited, for your account and for each address. Past the limit, nothing is sent, the code sent before stays valid, and Account activity lists Confirmation code not sent: limit reached.

    The page says What to do
    Wrong or expired code. Check it or ask for another one. Check the code, or press Send another code
    Too many codes sent. Try again later. Type the code you received last, or ask for another one later
    This link was already used, has expired or was replaced. Send a new code from your account. Press Send a code on Your information again

    A phone number you correct yourself is not verified until you confirm it.

    Encrypted email¶

    Beside an email address that is verified, Encrypted email opens the page where you give an S/MIME certificate or an OpenPGP public key: the email the sign-in service sends you is then encrypted to it. The page opens only when you signed in less than five minutes ago; otherwise the sign-in page comes first.

    The page says Emails sent to your address can be encrypted to your S/MIME certificate or OpenPGP key, which must carry this address. The sender, recipient, date and subject stay readable.

    Choose the Format, S/MIME or OpenPGP, paste the Certificate or public key, and press Encrypt my emails: S/MIME: the certificate alone, in PEM. OpenPGP: the public key alone, ASCII-armoured. Replaces the previous one. The page then says Your emails are encrypted with the format to the key and its fingerprint, followed by An email that cannot be encrypted is not sent. and, when the key has an end, Valid until its date. Stop encrypting my emails takes it away.

    The page says What to do
    This certificate or public key cannot be read. Paste the certificate or the key alone, in the form the format asks for
    This certificate or key cannot encrypt: it needs an RSA key of at least 2048 bits (S/MIME) or an encryption subkey (OpenPGP). Give a certificate or a key made for encryption
    This certificate or key has expired, is revoked or is not valid yet. Give one that is valid today
    This certificate or key does not carry your verified address. Give one made for the address shown on the page
    Encryption needs a verified email address, and your account has none. Confirm your email address first

    The email that carries a confirmation code is never encrypted.
