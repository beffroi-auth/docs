# Your account

Your organisation gives you an account on its sign-in service. That account is what signs you in to the
applications of your organisation: an application that needs to know who you are sends you to the sign-in
pages, and they send you back to it once you have proved who you are.

The sign-in pages show the logo of your organisation when it has one, its name beside the page on a wide screen,
and draw their buttons in its colours when it chose some.

## Reaching your account

Open the address your organisation gives you for its sign-in service in a browser. If that browser is not
signed in, the pages ask you to [sign in](signing-in.md) first, then open **Your account**.

**Your account** is the home of your account: one card per page, each saying what the page is for and what your
account holds there. A column beside every page lists the others; on a narrow screen, the menu button in the bar
at the top shows it. Your username, in the same bar, opens a menu with **Your information** and **Sign out**.

Opened in a browser that is not signed in, **Pending requests** takes you to the sign-in page, and the other pages of
your account say **Sign in, then open this page again.**, with a **Sign in** link.

## The pages

The column lists the pages under three headings, **Personal data**, **Security** and **Activity**, with
**Pending requests** above them.

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| [Pending requests](requests.md) | Requests waiting for your answer. |
| [Your information](information.md) | View and correct your information. |
| [Your data](your-data.md) | Download a copy of your data. |
| [Your password](password.md) | Change your password. |
| [Your passkeys](passkeys.md) | Sign in with your device, without a password. |
| [Two-factor authentication](two-factor.md) | A code on top of your password. |
| [Notifications](notifications.md) | How you receive your notifications. |
| [Open sessions](sessions.md) | The devices signed in to your account. |
| [Allowed applications](applications.md) | What each application obtains from you. |
| [Account activity](activity.md) | Sign-ins and changes to your account. |

[Signing in](signing-in.md) says what the sign-in pages ask, and how to choose their language.

## What every page shares

- **Your organisation decides what you can do.** Which ways of signing in it allows, which of your details you
  may change and which messages it can send you are its choices, and the pages offer only what it allows.
- **A table shows twenty lines at a time.** The fields above it narrow what it shows: **Filter** applies them,
  and **Reset**, shown once they narrow it, brings the table back as it first opened.
- **Dates are written in your time zone** when your account names one under **Time zone** on
  [Your information](information.md), and the zone is always written after the time.
